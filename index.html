How it works

From closet chaos to outfit-ready in 4 steps

No complicated setup — Closeta builds your digital wardrobe as you go.

1 Snap or upload

Photograph each clothing item or import from your gallery — Closeta builds your closet as you add pieces.

2 Tag & organize

Sort by category, color, season and occasion into cabinets, categories and subcategories you control.

3 Mix & save outfits

Combine tops, bottoms, shoes and accessories into complete looks, or swipe to discover new combinations.

4 Plan & pack

Schedule outfits on the planner calendar and auto-generate packing lists before every trip.

FAQ

Questions people ask before downloading

What is Closeta and how does it work? +

Closeta is a wardrobe organizer and outfit planner app. You photograph or upload your clothes, tag them by category and color, then build outfits by mixing tops, bottoms, shoes and accessories. You can plan outfits on a calendar and generate packing lists for trips.

Learn more about organizing your closet digitally →
Is Closeta free to use? +

Yes. Closeta is free to download with core wardrobe organizing and outfit planning features included. An optional Premium subscription (monthly or yearly) unlocks additional styling features.

Learn more about what's included →
Does Closeta store my photos in the cloud? +

No. Closeta is privacy-first and offline by design — your wardrobe photos and data are stored on your device, not uploaded to a mandatory cloud service, and are never sold or shared.

Read the full privacy policy →
Can Closeta help me build a capsule wardrobe? +

Yes. Closeta lets you catalog every piece you own, see what you actually wear, and mix and match items to build a smaller, more versatile capsule wardrobe over time.

Learn how to build a capsule wardrobe →
Does Closeta create packing lists for travel? +

Yes. Create a trip in Closeta, assign an outfit to each day, and the app automatically generates a packing list from the clothing items used in those outfits.

Learn more about travel packing lists →
Is Closeta available on Android? +

Closeta is currently available on iPhone and iPad through the Apple App Store. An Android version is not available yet — join the App Store waitlist notifications to hear when that changes.
