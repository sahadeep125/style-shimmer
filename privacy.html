Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Closeta ("we," "our," "the app") is a wardrobe organizer and outfit planner app developed by Deep Saha. This policy explains what information Closeta collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. Closeta is built to be privacy-first: your wardrobe photos and data are designed to stay on your device, not on our servers.

1. What Closeta stores on your device

When you use Closeta, the following data is created and stored locally on your device:

  • Photos of your clothing items that you take or import from your photo library
  • Item details you enter — category, color, season, occasion, and any names or notes
  • Outfits you build, save, like, or skip
  • Planner calendar entries and trip/packing list data

This data is not uploaded to a Closeta-operated server as part of normal app use. It remains on your device unless you explicitly choose to export or share it (for example, exporting an outfit image to send to a friend).

2. Photo and camera access

Closeta requests access to your device's camera and photo library only so you can add pictures of your clothing items. We do not access, scan, or transmit any photos beyond the ones you explicitly select or capture within the app. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device's Settings, though doing so will limit the app's ability to add new item photos.

3. Account information

Closeta does not require you to create an account to use its core features. If future versions introduce optional cloud backup or sign-in, that will be clearly opt-in, and this policy will be updated to describe what's collected and why.

4. In-app purchases

Closeta offers an optional Premium subscription (monthly or yearly), processed entirely through Apple's App Store in-app purchase system. Closeta does not receive or store your payment card details — that information is handled directly by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. We may receive non-identifying purchase status (e.g., "subscription active") from Apple to unlock Premium features.

5. Analytics

Our website (this landing page, not the app itself) uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns, such as which pages are visited and which links are clicked. This helps us improve the site and doesn't include your wardrobe or app data. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using your browser's do-not-track settings or a browser extension.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not share your wardrobe photos or item data with advertisers.
  • We do not require a mandatory cloud account to use Closeta's core features.
  • We do not use your clothing photos for any purpose beyond displaying them back to you inside the app.

7. Data deletion

Because your wardrobe data lives on your device, you can delete it at any time by removing individual items within the app or by uninstalling Closeta entirely, which removes all locally stored app data along with it. If you'd like help understanding what data exists or how to remove it, contact us at the email below.

8. Children's privacy

Closeta is rated 4+ and is not directed at collecting personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children beyond what a parent or guardian chooses to enter while using the app on a shared device.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy as Closeta's features evolve. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact us

Questions about this privacy policy or how Closeta handles your data can be sent to sahadeep125@gmail.com.
